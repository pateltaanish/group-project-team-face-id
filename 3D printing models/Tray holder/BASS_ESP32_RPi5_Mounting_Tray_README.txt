BASS Project ESP32 + CanaKit Raspberry Pi 5 Mounting Tray

Tray size: 172 x 165 mm
Base thickness: 3 mm
Perimeter lip height: 6 mm

Placement zones:
- ESP32 enclosure: approximately 152 x 55.8 mm including its external mounting tabs
- CanaKit / Raspberry Pi 5 cage: approximately 98.5 x 70.3 mm

The tray has low placement guides around both devices, a center divider, and
four optional 4.2 mm corner holes for mounting the tray itself. The center of
each placement area is left flat for Velcro or adhesive.

The Pi cage dimensions are approximate. Test-fit the actual CanaKit cage before
printing a final production tray because case variants and manufacturing
tolerances can differ.

Recommended print settings:
- PLA or PETG
- 0.20 mm layer height
- 3-4 wall lines
- 15-25% infill
- Print flat on the tray bottom
